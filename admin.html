ICOLD 2027 Survey — Responses

This survey saves every response through Netlify's built-in Forms feature, so there's no separate admin password here — responses are viewed inside the Netlify dashboard for the site (visible only to whoever is logged into that Netlify account):

  1. Log in at app.netlify.com
  2. Open this site → Forms tab
  3. Click icold2027-survey to see every submission
  4. Use Export to CSV to download all responses

A custom statistics dashboard (bar charts per question, like the original design) can be added on top of this once the site is claimed under a Netlify account — just ask.
